Build your army, upgrade units and win tactical battles in this fantasy strategy game. Master your strategy and crush the enemy base!

About the game

Every unit matters — every decision can turn the tide of battle.

⚔️ Build your army

Recruit swordsmen, shield warriors, archers, mages and powerful creatures — each with unique strengths and weaknesses. Discover winning unit combinations.

🛡️ Fight tactical battles

Summon units and watch them fight automatically. Manage resources, counter enemy troops and cast spells at the right moment.

⬆️ Upgrade your units

Collect unit cards, upgrade your warriors and improve their battle stats with every victory.

🏰 Strengthen your base

Upgrade your base and abilities to prepare for tougher challenges and stronger enemies.

🔥 Conquer new challenges

Face different enemy armies, experiment with new tactics and find a better strategy in every fight.

🎯 Easy to play, fun to master

Simple controls, quick battles and colorful fantasy combat with real tactical depth.
