Privacy Policy for Battle Burst

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Developer: Indie Mage (IndieMage)
Website: indiemage.com
Contact: indiemagestudio@gmail.com

1. Scope

Indie Mage ("we," "us," or "our") develops Battle Burst (the "Game"). This policy explains the information processed in connection with the Game, including through Google Analytics for Firebase and, in Android builds containing advertising, Appodeal and its advertising partners.

The Game does not currently provide separate player account registration, Google Play Games sign-in, or cloud-save synchronization. Real-money purchases are not currently enabled. Appodeal was introduced in Android build 2; earlier builds do not contain this advertising integration. Test builds currently use Appodeal's test-ad mode. The advertising disclosures below apply to builds containing the advertising SDK, including test builds.

2. Gameplay data stored on your device

The Game stores progress and preferences locally, including completed stages, player level, unlocked units, cards, upgrades, virtual currencies, rewards, and local gameplay counters. Selected gameplay information is also sent as analytics events, as described below. The Game does not upload your complete save file as a cloud backup.

3. Google Analytics for Firebase

The Android version uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand gameplay, balance difficulty and the virtual economy, and improve the Game.

  • Gameplay events: battle starts, results and exits, stage identifiers, attempts, battle duration, selected units and equipment, player level, upgrades, rewards, and virtual-currency earnings, spending and balances. Events can include generated battle or gameplay-transaction identifiers; these are not payment-card details.
  • App and device information: app-instance identifiers, sessions and app interactions, app version, device model, operating system, language, and related technical information collected by the SDK.
  • Approximate location and network information: Google processes IP addresses in providing Analytics and can derive approximate location, such as country or city. The Game does not request GPS or precise device location.
  • Advertising identifiers: the Analytics SDK can collect the Android advertising identifier where available and permitted by device settings and applicable consent requirements, even when the Game does not display advertisements.

We do not intentionally send your name, email address, or full save file in gameplay analytics events. Analytics identifiers still allow events from an app installation to be associated and should not be regarded as completely anonymous.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase, and Google Analytics data collection information.

4. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to support and privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

5. How information is used and shared

We use information to operate the Game, keep local progress, understand gameplay, improve balance and reliability, deliver and measure rewarded advertisements in builds containing advertising, and respond to requests. Analytics information is processed by Google and its service providers. Advertising information may be shared with Appodeal and participating advertising partners for the purposes in section 6, subject to applicable service terms, privacy practices, settings, and consent requirements. Support messages are processed through our email service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, security, or legal rights.

6. Purchases and advertising

Current shop transactions use virtual game resources. Offers showing real-money prices are unavailable until a payment service is connected; the current Game does not collect real-money payment transactions or payment-card information.

Planned real-money purchases: a forthcoming Android version is intended to offer optional digital-content purchases through Google Play Billing. Once enabled, Google will process payments, and the Game may process product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, and information needed to verify purchases, deliver content, and restore eligible purchases. We will not receive full payment-card details from Google Play Billing. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Appodeal advertising: Android builds containing Appodeal use its SDK to support optional rewarded advertisements. Appodeal and participating advertising partners may collect or process IP addresses and advertising identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), other device identifiers, app and device information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad requests, impressions, clicks, and other ad interactions.

This information supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and advertising personalization where permitted by applicable requirements and user choices. SDK initialization and test-ad requests can involve data processing even if no advertisement is ultimately displayed. Test-ad mode should not be understood as disabling all SDK data collection. The Game does not request GPS or precise device location.

The Android integration includes adapters for Google AdMob, AppLovin, BidMachine, BIGO Ads, DT Exchange (Digital Turbine), InMobi, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Unity Ads, Liftoff/Vungle, and Yandex Mobile Ads. Inclusion of an adapter does not mean that every network serves every player. Participation and processing depend on the build, network availability, service configuration, region, and applicable privacy choices.

See Appodeal's Privacy Policy, Appodeal's partner privacy policies and privacy controls, BidMachine's Privacy Policy, and BIGO Ads' Privacy Policy. Appodeal also provides advertising opt-out information. Advertising consent prompts and options depend on the release, location, and service configuration; the current test integration does not provide a separate in-game advertising privacy-settings screen. You can contact us about your choices or privacy requests at the address below.

7. Storage, retention, and security

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is removed or replaced. Android backup and restore behavior may depend on your device and Google account settings.

Analytics information is retained according to the Google Analytics property's data-retention settings and Google's applicable policies. Retention controls for user-level and event-level data do not necessarily remove aggregate reports. See Google Analytics data retention.

Advertising information processed by Appodeal and advertising partners is retained according to their applicable policies, the purpose of processing, and legal obligations. Their privacy policies linked in section 6 explain their retention practices and how to submit requests.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and associated follow-up or legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

We use reasonable safeguards and limit access to information under our control. Firebase encrypts Analytics data in transit using HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data deletion and privacy requests

To remove local progress and preferences, use Android Settings, open Apps, select Battle Burst, and choose Storage, then Clear storage or Clear data. Names vary by device. This resets local progress and cannot be undone unless a device backup restores it. Clearing local data does not delete analytics information already sent to Google.

For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning data under our control, email indiemagestudio@gmail.com with the subject Battle Burst Privacy Request. Describe the request and any relevant app information. We may need additional information to verify and locate the relevant records. Because the Game does not link analytics to your email address or a player account, we may be unable to identify a particular installation from an email address alone. We will explain any identification or deletion limitations when responding.

Google account and Google Play records are managed by Google through its own privacy controls. Deleting local Game data does not delete your Google account or Google's platform records.

Deleting local Game data also does not automatically delete records already processed by advertising providers. You may submit requests to Appodeal and its partners using the contacts and controls in their privacy policies, or contact us for assistance with information under our control.

9. Privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us using the address above to make a request.

You can manage available advertising-ID controls in Android settings. Resetting or deleting an advertising ID does not stop all Analytics collection. The current version does not provide an in-game Analytics preference screen.

10. Children's privacy

Battle Burst is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The minimum age for providing valid consent to particular data processing may be higher under local law.

Children's personal information requires particular care. The Game does not request a child's name, email address, or contact details through registration. The Analytics processing described above may nevertheless involve device or app identifiers and gameplay activity.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information in connection with Battle Burst, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

11. International processing

Google and other service providers may process information outside your country of residence. Applicable safeguards and transfer arrangements are described in their privacy policies and service terms.

12. Changes and contact

We will publish changes on this page and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before new processing begins.

For questions about Battle Burst or this policy, contact Indie Mage (IndieMage) at indiemagestudio@gmail.com.
